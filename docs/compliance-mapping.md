## 1. Architectural Invariants & Scope

BitGuard is a defensive signing firewall operating as an independent **policy decision point** coupled with an isolated **enforcement signer shim**. 

- **Containment & Risk Reduction:** BitGuard **reduces and contains** unauthorized transaction signing across exchange hot wallets.
- **Latency Target:** Operating performance is **target p99 under 15 ms, measured**.
- **Deployment Status:** **Production-ready architecture; pilot after audit and shadow-mode validation**.
- **Data Provenance:** Every metric, record, and report export is tagged with explicit data provenance labels: `[REAL]`, `[SYNTHETIC]`, or `[REPORTED]` (Invariant #8).
- **Cryptographic Assurance:** Every report is stamped with an immutable, hash-chained audit trail verification status (Invariant #7) anchored to external storage receipts.

## 2. Standard Compliance Evidence Reports (F20)

BitGuard provides three automated compliance evidence reports available via `/v1/reports/*` with `Compliance` role access:

1. **Incident Post-Mortem Report (`/v1/reports/incident/{id}`):**
   - **Contents:** Detailed timeline of security events, initial detection timestamp, containment timestamp, exact time-to-contain in milliseconds, automated runbook actions executed, checklist completion with actor identities, signing decisions evaluated during the incident window, and policy manifest version in effect at the time of the incident.
   - **Formats:** Cryptographically stamped PDF and structured CSV.
   - **Data Provenance:** Stamped with `[REAL]` or `[SYNTHETIC]` and full audit-chain validation receipt.

2. **Monthly Control & Decision Report (`/v1/reports/monthly`):**
   - **Contents:** Aggregated signing decision counts classified by verdict (`ALLOW`, `HOLD`, `DENY`), quarantine hold ticket resolution statistics (approved, denied, expired to deny, released), false-positive count and percentage, containment latency percentiles (mean, p50, p99), dual-control hardware WebAuthn approval counts and unique approver counts, circuit breaker state transition history with epoch bumps, and dead-man fail-safe heartbeat uptime / availability tracking.
   - **Formats:** Cryptographically stamped PDF and structured CSV.
   - **Data Provenance:** Stamped with `[REAL]`, `[REPORTED]`, and `[SYNTHETIC]` provenance.

3. **Policy Change & Governance History (`/v1/reports/policy-history`):**
   - **Contents:** Monotonic policy version history, author identity, asymmetric change classification (`TIGHTEN` vs `LOOSEN`), cryptographic content SHA-256 hash, dual-control hardware WebAuthn approver list, mandatory cool-down expiration timestamps, attached backtest evaluation report references, and human-readable rule and threshold diffs.
   - **Formats:** Cryptographically stamped PDF and structured CSV.
   - **Data Provenance:** Stamped with `[REAL]` and audit-chain integrity proof.

## 5. Auditor Verification Checklist

When reviewing BitGuard compliance evidence, auditors can independently verify:
1. **Cryptographic Chain Validity:** Execute `GET /v1/audit/verify` or check the `AUDIT CHAIN STAMP` in exported PDF/CSV headers.
2. **Replay Rejection:** Ensure that approval token nonces are strictly consumed once and replay attacks fail closed.
3. **Data Provenance:** Confirm that figures originating from integration or testing environments are clearly marked `[SYNTHETIC]` or `[REPORTED]` and not confused with `[REAL]` testnet production-like traffic.
4. **Separation of Duties:** Verify that individuals submitting signing requests cannot self-approve quarantine hold tickets.
